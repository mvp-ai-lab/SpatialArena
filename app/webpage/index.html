SpatialArena at a Glance

What the benchmark is made of: its evaluation cases, the conditions its datasets test, and the models it scores

456 evaluation cases by environment, motion, scene type, and dataset

Rings, from the inside: environment, static or dynamic scenes, and scene type. Outer bars: evaluation cases per dataset (square-root length), stacked by task. Each scene is placed once: ETH3D and Tanks & Temples are split by scene, and the Marigold suite by subset (NYUv2 indoor, KITTI driving, ETH3D and DIODE mixed). SLAM counts each sequence once per range track it runs in. SpatialArena (highlighted) counts one case per MonoDepth scene, VideoDepth clip, MVRecon or SfM scene, and SLAM sequence. A case is dynamic when people or vehicles cover over 3% of the pixels its camera sees (or appear in over 60% of its frames).

Our scenes

The 30 SpatialArena scenes, captured with a handheld 3-camera LiDAR rig: each frame with its RGB image, the LiDAR depth projected into it, and the dense depth rendered from the LiDAR map.

Depth colours: turbo, nearer is warmer; the LiDAR and dense maps of a frame share one range. People and vehicles are masked out of the images. Pose-only SLAM sequences have no depth on disk.

Depth alignment

Most models predict depth only up to a scale and shift. SpatialArena fits one scale and shift per image (per sequence for video depth) on the pixels with ground truth: least squares to start, then Huber reweighting until the fit stops moving, so bad pixels lose weight instead of dragging the fit. Trajectory alignment

Predicted cameras live in their own frame, up to a similarity transform. SpatialArena fits a robust Sim(3): pose-aware two-frame samples, a refit on the best sample's inliers, then Cauchy-weighted refits on every frame. Each case is one trial of our alignment study, run through the evaluator's own code.

                        Five datasets serve two tasks and are packaged once: the other task's folder only holds a

    cd /data/spatialarena
for pair in MVRecon/eth3d:SfM/eth3d MVRecon/tanks_and_temples:SfM/tanks_and_temples \
            SfM/dtu:MVRecon/dtu SfM/hiroom:MVRecon/hiroom SfM/scannetpp:MVRecon/scannetpp; do
    link=${pair%%:*}; data=${pair#*:}
    if [ -f "$link/SEE_INSTEAD.md" ] && [ -d "$data" ]; then rm -r "$link" && ln -s "../$data" "$link"; fi
done
